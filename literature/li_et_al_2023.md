# Associations Between Dietary and Blood Inflammatory Indices and Their Effects on Cognitive Function in Elderly Americans

- **Authors**: Wanyue Li et al.
- **Year**: 2023
- **Journal**: Frontiers in Neuroscience, 17:1117056
- **DOI**: 10.3389/fnins.2023.1117056
- **PubMed**: 36895419
- **Data**: NHANES 2011-2014, ≥60 years (n=2,479)

## Key Findings
- Composite cognitive Z-score from CERAD-WL, CERAD-DR, AF, DSST
- DII + blood inflammatory indices: WBC, NE, NLR, PLR, NAR, SII, SIRI
- DII negatively correlated with composite Z-score
- Higher DII with NLR, NAR, SII, SIRI all increased cognitive impairment risk
- DII positively correlated with SII in cognitively impaired group
- Combined dietary + blood inflammation markers give better risk assessment

## Relevance to Our Study
NHANES 2011-2014. Extends DII alone to combined dietary+blood inflammation markers. Confirms inflammation pathway. Does NOT examine PNI/CONUT/GNRI or depression mediation.
