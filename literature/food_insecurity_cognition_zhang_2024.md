# Impact of Food Insecurity on Cognitive Health in Older Adults: Insights from the NHANES 2011-2014 Data

- **Authors**: Yanwei Zhang et al.
- **Year**: 2024
- **Journal**: Frontiers in Nutrition, 11:1421970
- **DOI**: 10.3389/fnut.2024.1421970
- **Data**: NHANES 2011-2014, ≥60 years (n=2,915)

## Key Findings
- Food insecurity → lower cognitive scores: IRT (β=-0.94), DRT (β=-0.37), DSST (β=-3.83)
- AFT not significant (β=-0.39, p=0.29)
- Dose-response: very low food security worst outcomes
- Moderation by smoking and alcohol use
- Food insecure group: younger, lower % non-Hispanic White, higher BMI, lower PIR, lower education

## Relevance to Our Study
**Gap-filling** — Identifies food security as upstream social determinant of both nutrition and cognition in same NHANES 2011-2014. Suggests food security could be upstream modifier of nutritional indices. No study has tested food security × nutritional indices interaction on cognition.
