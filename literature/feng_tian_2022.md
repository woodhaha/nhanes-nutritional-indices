# The Association Between Dietary Inflammatory Index and Cognitive Performance in Older Adults Aged 60 Years and Older

- **Authors**: Yijun Feng, Changwei Tian
- **Year**: 2022
- **Journal**: Frontiers in Nutrition, 9:748000
- **DOI**: 10.3389/fnut.2022.748000
- **Data**: NHANES 2011-2014, adults ≥60 (n=2,780-2,859)

## Key Findings
- DII calculated from 27 nutritional parameters
- Cognitive tests: DSST, CERAD-WL, CERAD-DR, Animal Fluency (AF)
- Comparing highest vs lowest DII tertile:
  - DSST: OR=1.97 (1.08-3.58), per 1-unit: OR=1.17 (1.01-1.38)
  - AF: OR=1.76 (1.30-2.37), per 1-unit: OR=1.17 (1.05-1.29)
  - CERAD-WL and CERAD-DR: not significant
- Nonlinear dose-response: association significant only when DII >3.0, then rose steeply
- Associations consistent in both men and women

## Relevance to Our Study
Direct NHANES 2011-2014 DII+cognition paper. Uses same dataset/population as our study. Provides benchmark for DII effect size on DSST and AF. Did NOT examine PNI/CONUT/GNRI or depression mediation.
