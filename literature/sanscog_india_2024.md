# Nutritional Deficits and their Impact on Brain Morphology and Cognition in a Southern Indian Rural Community

- **Authors**: CBR-SANSCOG Study Group
- **Year**: 2024
- **Journal**: Not specified (PMC12742256)
- **DOI**: N/A (PMCID: PMC12742256)
- **Data**: CBR-SANSCOG cohort, India, ≥45 years (n=3,687; MRI subset n=1,150)

## Key Findings
- Compared GNRI, PNI, CONUT, BMI, MUAC for cognition
- Malnutrition prevalence: MUAC 8.8%, GNRI 9%, PNI 15%, CONUT 19%
- GNRI had best agreement with Standard Combined Index (SCI)
- GNRI, BMI, MUAC positively associated with cognitive performance
- PNI and CONUT: no association with cognition or brain volumes
- MUAC had stronger relationship with cognition than BMI
- GNRI associated with brain ROI volumes on 3T MRI

## Relevance to Our Study
**Only community-based study comparing all 4 indices (GNRI/PNI/CONUT/BMI) for cognition.** Found GNRI superior, PNI and CONUT not associated. But: Indian population, different cognitive tests, younger (mean 57 years). GAP: needs replication in US elderly NHANES population.
