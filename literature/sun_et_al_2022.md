# Cognitive Impairment Mediates the Association Between Dietary Inflammation and Depressive Symptoms in the Elderly

- **Authors**: Mengzi Sun, Ling Wang, Yueyang Hu, Xuhan Wang, Shoumeng Yan, Yinpei Guo, Jing Li, Zechun Xie, Bo Li
- **Year**: 2022
- **Journal**: Nutrients, 14(23):5118
- **DOI**: 10.3390/nu14235118
- **Data**: NHANES 2011-2014, ≥60 years (n=2,550)

## Key Findings
- Examined DII → cognition → depression pathway
- Cognitive impairment measured by CERAD-immediate, CERAD-delayed, AF, DSST
- Depression measured by PHQ-9
- Proinflammatory diet + cognitive impairment both risk factors for depressive symptoms
- ALL four cognitive dimensions mediated the DII-depression association
- Proportion of mediation: 10.0%-36.7% across cognitive tests
- DSST showed highest mediation proportion (36.7%)
- Interaction between DII and cognitive impairment (P-interaction=0.060)

## Relevance to Our Study
**Highly relevant** — Tests cognition as mediator between DII and depression (opposite direction to our proposed model). Same NHANES 2011-2014. Establishes that cognition-depression-DII pathways exist. GAP: Our study would test NUTRITIONAL INDICES (DII/PNI/CONUT/GNRI) → cognition, with depression as mediator (vs their DII → cognition → depression). Still confirms the interconnectedness of these three domains.
