# Depression and Cognition Are Associated with Lipid Dysregulation

- **Authors**: Not specified
- **Year**: 2024
- **Journal**: Translational Psychiatry, 14:128
- **DOI**: 10.1038/s41398-024-02847-6
- **Data**: NHANES (specific cycles not fully clear)

## Key Findings
- Depression mediates HDL → cognition relationship
- Significant natural indirect effect of depression on DSST (p=0.037), AFT (p=0.049), DWRT (p=0.049)
- HDL protective effect on cognition partially mediated through depressive symptoms
- Suggests common pathway: biomarkers → depression → cognition

## Relevance to Our Study
Supports the depression-as-mediator framework in biomarker→cognition research. Different exposure (HDL) but same mediation model structure. Confounds like age, sex, BMI adjusted.
