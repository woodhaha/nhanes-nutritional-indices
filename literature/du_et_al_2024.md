# DII + Physical Activity + Cognitive Function

- **Authors**: Du et al.
- **Year**: 2024
- **Journal**: General Hospital Psychiatry
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.genhosppsych.2024.09.003
- **Data**: NHANES

## Key Findings
- Examined joint effects of DII and physical activity on cognitive function
- Interaction between pro-inflammatory diet and physical inactivity
- Combined risk assessment for cognitive impairment

## Relevance to Our Study
Extends DII-cognition research by adding lifestyle modifier (PA). Confirms importance of considering modifiers/moderators. Different from our proposed depression mediation model.
