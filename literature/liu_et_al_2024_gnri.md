# Association Between Lower Geriatric Nutritional Risk Index and Low Cognitive Functions in United States Older Adults

- **Authors**: Jiuling Liu et al.
- **Year**: 2024
- **Journal**: Frontiers in Nutrition, 11:1483790
- **DOI**: 10.3389/fnut.2024.1483790
- **Data**: NHANES 2011-2014, ≥60 years (n=2,925)

## Key Findings
- GNRI >98 = normal, ≤98 = malnutrition (233 malnourished)
- Malnutrition associated with increased risk of low cognitive function:
  - CERAD W-L: OR=1.68 (95%CI 1.19-2.36, p=0.003)
  - AFT: OR=1.74 (95%CI 1.26-2.41, p=0.009)
  - DSST: OR=1.63 (95%CI 1.11-2.38, p=0.012)
  - Composite Z-score: OR=1.87 (95%CI 1.29-2.71, p=0.001)
- Interaction with stroke history (p=0.020): GNRI effect strongest in stroke survivors (OR=5.92)
- Interaction with age≥70

## Relevance to Our Study
Second GNRI+cognition paper using same NHANES 2011-2014. Larger n than Tan (2024). Important finding: GNRI effect modified by stroke and age. Provides comparison effect sizes. No multi-index or depression mediation.
