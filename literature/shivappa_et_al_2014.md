# Designing and Developing a Literature-Derived, Population-Based Dietary Inflammatory Index

- **Authors**: Nitin Shivappa, Susan E. Steck, Thomas G. Hurley, James R. Hussey, James R. Hebert
- **Year**: 2014
- **Journal**: Public Health Nutrition, 17(8):1689-1696
- **DOI**: 10.1017/S1368980013002115
- **Data**: Literature-derived, validated against NHANES

## Key Findings
- DII developed from ~1,943 peer-reviewed articles on diet and inflammation
- 45 food parameters (later versions use 27-28 for NHANES)
- Validated against inflammatory markers (CRP, IL-6)
- Higher DII = more pro-inflammatory diet
- Population-based scoring using global referent database

## Relevance to Our Study
**Foundational paper** — DII methodology. Required citation for any DII study. Provides the scoring algorithm used in all above NHANES DII-cognition papers.
