# Association of the Geriatric Nutritional Risk Index with Cognitive Function Level and Cognitive Impairment Among Older Adults with Cardiometabolic Disease

- **Authors**: Xiaofan Miao, Fang Wang, Bixia Li, Yu Chen, Wenbin Shang
- **Year**: 2026
- **Journal**: Current Alzheimer Research (published online)
- **DOI**: 10.2174/0115672050426913260114053708
- **Data**: NHANES 2011-2014, ≥60 years with cardiometabolic disease (n=1,937)

## Key Findings
- GNRI + cognition specifically in cardiometabolic disease (diabetes, hypertension, stroke, CHF)
- Per 1-SD increase in GNRI: increased CERAD scores and AFT scores (β=0.34, 95%CI 0.11-0.57)
- Highest GNRI quartile vs lowest: OR=0.59 (95%CI 0.41-0.85) for cognitive impairment
- Linear dose-response (restricted cubic splines)
- Proposed mechanisms: albumin neuroprotection + BMI-mediated brain structural changes

## Relevance to Our Study
Confirms GNRI-cognition association in a cardiometabolic subgroup. Suggests GNRI's protective effect may be especially relevant in populations with metabolic disease. Same NHANES 2011-2014.
