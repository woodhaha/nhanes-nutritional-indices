# Food Insecurity Is Associated with Lower Cognitive Functioning in a National Sample of Older Adults

- **Authors**: E.T. Portela-Parra, C.W. Leung
- **Year**: 2019
- **Journal**: Journal of Nutrition, 149(10):1812-1817
- **DOI**: 10.1093/jn/nxz120
- **Data**: NHANES (cognitive function data)

## Key Findings
- Food insecurity associated with lower cognitive function in older adults
- Cross-sectional analysis
- Adjusted for demographic and socioeconomic confounders

## Relevance to Our Study
Supports food security as upstream factor for cognition. Would be relevant if we incorporate food security as modifier/covariate in our multi-index model.
