# Association Between Dietary Inflammatory Index and Cognitive Impairment Among American Elderly: A Cross-Sectional Study

- **Authors**: Zhang et al.
- **Year**: 2024
- **Journal**: Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience, 16:1371873
- **DOI**: 10.3389/fnagi.2024.1371873
- **Data**: NHANES 2011-2014, older adults (n=947)

## Key Findings
- DII categorized into quartiles, cognitive impairment defined as lowest quartile on CERAD, AF, DSST
- DII positively correlated with low cognitive performance on AFT (OR=1.15, 95%CI 1.02-1.28) and DSST (OR=1.38, 95%CI 1.13-1.68)
- Highest DII quartile vs lowest: AFT OR=1.89 (95%CI 1.05-3.38), DSST OR=4.30 (95%CI 1.85-9.98)
- Nonlinear relationship between DII and DSST decline (p for nonlinearity=0.016)
- DII effect stronger in females and those aged >75
- Dose-response relationship with executive function

## Relevance to Our Study
Same NHANES 2011-2014, confirms DII-cognition link with larger effect sizes in highest quartile. Supports nonlinear relationship. No multi-index comparison or depression mediation.
