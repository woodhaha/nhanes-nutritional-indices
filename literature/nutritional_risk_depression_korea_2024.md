# Associations of Nutritional Health Risk With Cognitive Function in Older Korean Adults: The Mediating Roles of Lower Body Strength and Depression

- **Authors**: Not specified
- **Year**: 2024
- **Journal**: PMC12719135
- **DOI**: N/A (PMCID: PMC12719135)
- **Data**: Korean older adults (n=9,885)

## Key Findings
- Nutritional health risk (NHR) ← Korean nutrition screening checklist
- Mediation model: NHR → cognitive function
- Depression mediated 17% of the total effect
- Lower body strength mediated 13%
- Direct effect of NHR on cognition = 70%
- Complete mediation pathways confirmed with bootstrap (5,000 samples)

## Relevance to Our Study
**Important parallel** — Tests depression as mediator between nutritional risk and cognition (same model structure as our proposed study). Large Korean sample. Shows depression mediation proportions (~17%). Different nutritional assessment tool (screening checklist vs PNI/CONUT/GNRI/DII). GAP: needs replication in US elderly with objective nutritional indices.
