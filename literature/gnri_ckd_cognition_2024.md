# Association Between Geriatric Nutritional Risk Index and Cognitive Function in Older Adults With/Without Chronic Kidney Disease

- **Authors**: Not specified
- **Year**: 2024
- **Journal**: Journal of the American Geriatrics Society (PMC11391018)
- **DOI**: N/A (PMCID: PMC11391018)
- **Data**: NHANES 2011-2014, ≥60 years (n=2,728)

## Key Findings
- GNRI positively associated with global cognitive Z-score: β=0.01 (95%CI 0.00-0.01)
- Association consistent across subgroups (gender, age, smoking, diabetes, hypertension, BMI<25, alcohol, non-Hispanic White)
- Linear relationship (no nonlinearity detected)
- No interaction with CKD status — GNRI effect similar in CKD and non-CKD

## Relevance to Our Study
Confirms GNRI-cognition linear association. Adds CKD specificity. Same NHANES 2011-2014 data. Suggests GNRI effect is robust across subgroups.
