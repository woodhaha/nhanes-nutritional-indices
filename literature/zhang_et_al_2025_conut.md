# Associations Between Nutritional Status and Cognitive Impairment in Older Adults: Results from the NHANES 2011-2014 Cycles

- **Authors**: Xuehe Zhang, Long Yang, Zhong Wang, Hanxiao Wang, Shiyu Nie, Chunxiu Zhao et al.
- **Year**: 2025
- **Journal**: Frontiers in Nutrition, 12:1571990
- **DOI**: 10.3389/fnut.2025.1571990
- **Data**: NHANES 2011-2014, 60-80 years (n=~2,758)

## Key Findings
- CONUT score (albumin + lymphocyte + cholesterol): normal (0-1) vs malnutrition (2-12)
- 53.6% had cognitive impairment
- CONUT score associated with cognitive impairment: OR=1.133 (95%CI 1.052-1.221, p=0.002)
- Malnutrition group: OR=1.259 (95%CI 1.039-1.524, p=0.021)
- L-shaped nonlinear association (p<0.001)
- CONUT associated with AFT and DSST but not CERAD in fully adjusted models
- Compared CONUT with PNI, GNRI, NRI — CONUT more practical due to categorical thresholds

## Relevance to Our Study
**Critical paper** — The only CONUT+cognition NHANES 2011-2014 study. Same dataset. Mentions PNI, GNRI, NRI as related tools but does not directly compare all four. GAP: no side-by-side comparison of DII+PNI+CONUT+GNRI. No depression mediation. Provides benchmark CONUT effect size.
