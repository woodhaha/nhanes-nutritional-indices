# Exploring the Role of Vitamin D in Cognitive Function: Mediation by Depression with Diabetes Modulation in Older U.S. Adults

- **Authors**: Chang Shu, Chenguang Zheng, Xin Du, Da Luo
- **Year**: 2024
- **Journal**: Frontiers in Nutrition, 11:1356071
- **DOI**: 10.3389/fnut.2024.1356071
- **Data**: NHANES 2011-2014, ≥60 years (n=2,745)

## Key Findings
- Depression mediates vitamin D → cognition relationship
- Mediation proportion: 10.6% overall, 20.9% in diabetes subgroup
- Interaction between diabetes and depression significant in mediation pathway
- Subgroup with strongest effect: diabetic individuals
- Supports depression-as-mediator framework in nutrition-cognition research

## Relevance to Our Study
**Key precedent for mediation methodology** — Shows depression mediates nutritional factor→cognition using NHANES 2011-2014 with survey-weighted mediation analysis and bootstrap validation. Direct methodological parallel for our proposed depression mediation model.
