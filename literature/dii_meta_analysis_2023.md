# Meta-Analysis of the Association Between Dietary Inflammatory Index and Cognitive Health

- **Authors**: Not specified
- **Year**: 2023
- **Journal**: Frontiers in Nutrition, 10:1104255
- **DOI**: 10.3389/fnut.2023.1104255
- **Data**: 12 studies, meta-analysis

## Key Findings
- Higher DII → increased risk of AD and MCI: OR=1.34 (95%CI 1.21-1.49)
- Global function impairment: OR=1.63 (95%CI 1.36-1.96)
- Verbal fluency impairment: β=0.18 (95%CI 0.08-0.42)
- No significant association with executive function or episodic memory
- Pooled evidence across 12 studies confirms DII-cognition link

## Relevance to Our Study
Provides meta-analytic context for DII-cognition effect size. Shows domain-specificity: global function and verbal fluency most affected; executive function/memory less so.
