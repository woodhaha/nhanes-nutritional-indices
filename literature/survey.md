# Literature Survey: Nutritional Indices and Cognitive Function in Elderly — NHANES 2011-2014

**Research Question**: In US adults ≥60 from NHANES 2011-2014, what is the association between multi-index nutritional status (DII/PNI/CONUT/GNRI) and cognitive function, and does depression mediate this relationship?

**Last Updated**: 2026-07-24
**Papers Found**: 21

---

## 1. DII (Dietary Inflammatory Index) + Cognition — NHANES 2011-2014

| Paper | Key Finding |
|-------|-------------|
| **Feng & Tian (2022)** | DII→DSST OR=1.97 (T3 vs T1); nonlinear above DII>3.0 |
| **Li et al. (2023)** | DII+blood inflammation markers (NLR,SII,SIRI) → worse cognition |
| **Frith et al. (2018)** | DII inversely associated with episodic, semantic, working memory |
| **Zhang et al. (2024)** | DII Q4→DSST OR=4.30; effect stronger in females & age>75 |

### Meta-Analyses
| Paper | Key Finding |
|-------|-------------|
| **DII Meta (2023)** | DII→AD/MCI OR=1.34 (95%CI 1.21-1.49); global impairment OR=1.63 |

**Consensus**: Higher DII (pro-inflammatory diet) consistently associated with worse cognitive function, especially DSST and Animal Fluency. Effect modified by age and sex.

## 3. GNRI (Geriatric Nutritional Risk Index) + Cognition

| Paper | Population | Key Finding |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **Tan, Nie & Yan (2024)** | NHANES 2011-2014, n=2,592 | Higher GNRI → better cognition; possible ceiling effect |
| **Liu et al. (2024)** | NHANES 2011-2014, n=2,925 | Low GNRI→CERAD OR=1.68, AF OR=1.74, DSST OR=1.63 |
| **Miao et al. (2026)** | NHANES 2011-2014, n=1,937 | GNRI Q4→cognition OR=0.59 in cardiometabolic disease |
| **GNRI+CKD (2024)** | NHANES 2011-2014, n=2,728 | GNRI linear association; robust across subgroups |

**Consensus**: Higher GNRI = better cognition. Effect robust across subgroups. May interact with stroke history.

## 4. CONUT (Controlling Nutritional Status) + Cognition

| Paper | Population | Key Finding |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **Zhang et al. (2025)** | NHANES 2011-2014, n≈2,758 | CONUT→cognition OR=1.133; L-shaped nonlinear; malnutrition OR=1.259 |

**Status**: Only one NHANES CONUT+cognition paper identified. CONUT independently associated with cognitive impairment, especially AF and DSST.

## 5. Multi-Index Comparisons

| Paper | Population | Key Finding |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **Wang et al. (2024)** | Schizophrenia, China (n=235) | Only PNI significant (r=0.15); GNRI & CONUT not significant |
| **SANSCOG India (2024)** | Indian community, ≥45 (n=3,687) | GNRI best agreement; PNI & CONUT not associated with cognition |

**CRITICAL GAP**: No published study has compared all 4 indices (DII + PNI + CONUT + GNRI) head-to-head for cognitive function in the general US elderly population using NHANES 2011-2014.

## 6. Depression Mediation in Nutrition-Cognition Pathway

| Paper | Model | Key Finding |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **Sun et al. (2022)** | DII→cognition→depression (reverse direction) | Cognition mediates 10-36.7% of DII-depression association |
| **AHEI Diet Quality (2022)** | Diet quality→depression→cognition | Depression mediates 9-14% of diet-cognition link |
| **Shu et al. (2024)** | Vitamin D→depression→cognition | Depression mediates 10.6% overall; 20.9% in diabetes |
| **HDL → cognition (2024)** | HDL→depression→cognition | Depression mediates HDL's effect on cognition |
| **Korean elderly (2024)** | Nutrition risk→depression→cognition | Depression mediates 17% of nutrition-cognition link |

**CRITICAL GAP**: No study has tested depression as mediator between DII/PNI/CONUT/GNRI and cognitive function in NHANES. Multiple precedents support this pathway (depression consistently mediates nutrition→cognition at 9-21%).

## Summary of Evidence Gaps

### Gap 1: No head-to-head comparison of all 4 indices
DII (dietary inflammation), PNI, CONUT, and GNRI (objective nutritional biomarkers) have each been separately associated with cognitive function in NHANES 2011-2014. **No study has compared all four simultaneously** to determine which index is most strongly associated with cognitive function, or whether they capture independent or overlapping variance.

### Gap 2: No depression mediation test for nutritional indices → cognition
Depression has been established as a mediator in diet→cognition (AHEI, Vitamin D) and nutrition→cognition (Korean NHRS) pathways. **No study has tested depression as mediator** for DII→cognition, PNI→cognition, CONUT→cognition, or GNRI→cognition in US elderly.

### Gap 3: No food security × nutritional index interaction
Food insecurity is associated with both worse nutritional status and worse cognition. **No study has tested food security as upstream modifier** of the nutritional index-cognition relationship.

### Gap 4: No multi-index composite score
No study has constructed a multi-domain nutritional risk score combining dietary inflammation (DII) with objective nutritional biomarkers (PNI/CONUT/GNRI) for cognitive outcomes.
