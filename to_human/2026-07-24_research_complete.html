Biological coherence: Only indices containing albumin (PNI, GNRI) show depression-mediated pathways. Albumin is a systemic health marker that correlates with mood. DII (dietary recall) and CONUT (no albumin weighting) lack this pathway. Consistent with AHEI (10.5%) and Korean NHRS (17%) mediation literature.
