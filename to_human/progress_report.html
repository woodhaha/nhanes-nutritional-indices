Critical Findings

  • Time-dependent: PNI effect strongest in first 2 years post-diagnosis (HR 0.34), then attenuates
  • Competing risks: Protection is against non-cancer death (HR 0.63), not GI cancer death (HR 1.06)
  • Mechanism: Albumin drives the effect; lymphocyte count contributes nothing independently
  • Dietary vs Biochemical: HEI-2015 null → biochemical nutrition status is the operative pathway
  • Optimal threshold: PNI <48.5 → median survival 6.6 vs 12.8 years (RMST Δ=1.97 years)
  • PAF: 13.4% of deaths potentially attributable to low PNI
